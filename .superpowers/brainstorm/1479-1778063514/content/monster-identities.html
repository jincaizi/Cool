Slime — Aggressive Swarm Tactics

Slime FSM

Idle ──timeout──▶ Patrol ──detects player──▶ Chase
  ▲                  ▲                          │
  │                  │              ┌───────────┤
  │                  │              │           │
  └─return to spawn─┘              ▼           ▼
                               Attack1 ◀──▶ Attack2
                                  │           │
                                  ▼           ▼
                         [on cooldown]   SenseSomething
                              │         (Taunt if player dodges)
                              ▼
                            Repeat
  • Fast, aggressive, low HP
  • Two attack variants (Attack01=quick bite, Attack02=leap slam)
  • After missed attack → Taunt animation
  • SenseSomething = wiggle + alert when player nearby but out of range
  • "Annoying" — designed to swarm in groups
TurtleShell — Defensive Tank

TurtleShell FSM

Idle ──timeout──▶ Patrol ──detects player──▶ Chase
  ▲                  ▲                          │
  │                  │              ┌───────────┤
  │                  │              │           │
  └─return to spawn─┘              ▼           ▼
                               Attack1      Defend
                                  │           │
                                  │    ┌──────┤
                                  │    │      │
                                  ▼    ▼      ▼
                              Attack2  DefendHit  Counter
                                        (block)   (after N blocks)
  • Slow, high HP, high defense
  • Attack01=spin attack, Attack02=headbutt
  • Defend = raise shell, block frontal damage
  • DefendHit = recoil animation when hit while defending
  • After 2-3 blocks → counter-attack with bonus damage
  • "Wall" — designed to hold ground alone

Key Design Decisions

A

Both designs as shown

Slime gets Taunt/SenseSomething flavor + 2 attack variants. TurtleShell gets Defend/DefendHit/Counter mechanic.

B

Simpler — single Attack, no Counter

Keep it lean. Slime uses only Attack01. TurtleShell defends but no counter mechanic. Faster to implement.

C

Give Slime a unique mechanic too

Slime could leave a slowing trail, split into a smaller slime on death, or have a charge-up leap. Match TurtleShell's complexity.
